How to use this handbook

  1. Read a chapterEach one fits in a short evening. Diagrams are drawn to be copied into your own notebook.
  2. Practise outsideSkills are learned with your hands, in the field, with someone who already knows them.
  3. Tick the skills checkEach chapter ends with skills you can tick off. Your ticks stay in this browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  4. Print what you needEvery page prints cleanly, without menus or colour backgrounds, to slip into a map case.

Before every trip

Pack the Ten Essentials, leave a trip plan with someone at home, check the weather and fire rules, and know who is carrying the first-aid kit.
