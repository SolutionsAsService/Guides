Find your way with a map and compass, pack for the day, tie the knots that matter, camp lightly and look after each other. Six chapters, each ending in a skills check you can tick off as you learn.

Chapters

  1. 1 Compass navigation Take and follow a bearing with a lensatic compass, find where you are, and map an area by pacing. 7 skills
  2. 2 Reading a topographic map Scale, symbols and contour lines: read the shape of the land before you walk it. 6 skills
  3. 3 The Ten Essentials and trip planning What to carry on every outing, what to leave with someone at home, and how to keep water safe. 4 skills
  4. 4 Knots and rope Seven knots that cover nearly every job in camp, with what each is for and how to tie it. 7 skills
  5. 5 Camping and Leave No Trace Pick a safe site, store food, handle fire and stoves, and leave the place better than you found it. 6 skills
  6. 6 First aid in the field A refresher for the problems scouts meet most outdoors. It is not a replacement for a course. 6 skills

How to use this handbook

  1. Read a chapterEach one fits in a short evening. Diagrams are drawn to be copied into your own notebook.
  2. Practise outsideSkills are learned with your hands, in the field, with someone who already knows them.
  3. Tick the skills checkEach chapter ends with skills you can tick off. Your ticks stay in this browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  4. Print what you needEvery page prints cleanly, without menus or colour backgrounds, to slip into a map case.

Before every trip

Pack the Ten Essentials, leave a trip plan with someone at home, check the weather and fire rules, and know who is carrying the first-aid kit.

Compass & Trail is an independent, open-source handbook. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any scouting organisation. Learn hands-on skills from a qualified leader, and take a certified first-aid course.
